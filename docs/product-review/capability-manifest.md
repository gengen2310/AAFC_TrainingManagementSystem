## What this program must not remove without explicit authorisation

Every route and table in `capability_manifest_current.json` is a live
capability as of this program's start. Per `.claude/rules/capability-preservation.md`
§1 and this program's own non-negotiable §1: none of the 258 routes or 58
tables may be removed, hidden, or have its behaviour silently changed without:
identifying the capability, identifying dependent roles, recording the
proposed impact, proving an equivalent/improved replacement, preserving data/
historical access, preserving a compatibility path where required, and (if
genuinely removing something) explicit user authorisation recorded as
`USER-AUTHORISED REMOVAL` in the gap register.

The one standing exception already on record: the Training Summary → Dashboard
merge authorised 2026-08-04 (see `.claude/rules/capability-preservation.md`'s
"Standing authorisation" note). No other removal is pre-authorised.
