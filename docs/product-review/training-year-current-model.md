# Training Year — current model

Baseline recorded 2026-08-28 against `origin/main` @ `161bcb4`.

Descriptive only. No recommendations — the reframe and target model are separate
documents, per the instruction's §3.

Complements `tms-pw-current-state.md`, which covers TMS↔PW integration broadly.
This one covers only the Training Year.

---

## 1. What the year currently represents

A **materialised database row**, not a calendar context.

`PlanningYear` (`backend/app/models/planning.py:32-63`) carries:

| column | notes |
|---|---|
| `unit_id` | nullable — NULL means a wing- or national-scoped year |
| `wing_id` | nullable |
| `year` | integer, NOT NULL |
| `name` | `String(120)`, **NOT NULL**, free text |
| `active_status` | boolean, default `True` |
| `created_by` / `updated_by` / `version` | audit and optimistic concurrency |

There is no `status`, no `draft`, no timezone. The year has no relationship to
the calendar beyond the `year` integer, which nothing validates against the dates
of its children.

## 2. What requires a materialised row

Effectively everything year-scoped. `_get_year_or_404`
(`backend/app/routers/planning.py:196`) is the gate, called by the year detail,
patch, parade-dates, holidays, anchors, term planner, long-range, conflicts,
missions, annual program, export and import endpoints — every `/years/{year_id}/…`
route resolves a UUID to a row or returns 404.

**Nine foreign keys point at `planning_years`**, three NOT NULL:

- `ParadeDate.planning_year_id` — NOT NULL
- `HolidayPeriod.planning_year_id` — NOT NULL
- `TrainingClass.training_year_id` — NOT NULL
- `PlanningNotice.planning_year_id`, `PlanningConflict`, `AnchorEvent`,
  `AnchorPrepPlan` and two others — nullable

So a year cannot be browsed, planned or written to unless a row already exists.

## 3. What `active_status` currently means

Three unrelated jobs on one boolean.

1. **Archive state.** `active_status=false` is this system's archive
   (`.claude/rules/capability-preservation.md`; the PATCH archive path at
   `planning.py:560`).
2. **Default-year selection.** Both frontends pick a year to show from among
   active rows.
3. **Setup completion.** `backend/app/routers/setup.py:71` computes
   `planning_year_active = bool(active_year_ids)`, and holidays and anchor
   review at lines 68 and 80 are gated on `active_year_ids` being non-empty. A
   squadron with no active year reads as "not set up".

It does **not** mean "current". Nothing compares it to today's date.

## 4. What archive currently means

Retirement of a row, reversible, and the only lifecycle transition a user has.
Archived rows keep their children — `parade_dates` and `holiday_periods` remain
attached — but drop out of every active-filtered query, so a year's data becomes
invisible without being deleted.

The partial unique index `uq_planning_years_unit_year_active`
(`planning.py:47-56`) is scoped to active rows deliberately, so that archiving a
badly set-up year and creating a replacement stays possible.

## 5. What rollover currently does

`POST /api/planning/years/{year_id}/rollover` — `rollover_year`,
`planning.py:3631`.

- Creates a new `PlanningYear` with **`active_status=True`**
  (`planning.py:3658`) — the squadron immediately holds two active years.
- Names it `f"{py.name} → {target_year}"` (`planning.py:3643`), producing values
  like `2026 Training Year → 2027`.
- Rejects a duplicate `(unit_id, year)` with HTTP 409.
- Copies holiday periods, shifting them by `year_delta`.
- Copies active training class definitions (CLASS-11 comment).
- Copies parade dates with `year_delta` applied, creating parade nights for them.

This is the mechanism that produces both the multi-active-year state and the
`→ 2027` naming seen in production.

## 6. What the create-year UI currently does

**TMS** (`connected-frontend/index.html`, `openNewYearModal` at ~15995) opens a
modal collecting a year number and a **free-text name**.

**Planning Workspace** (`SetupPanel.tsx:75`) pre-fills the name as
`` `${new Date().getFullYear()}–${new Date().getFullYear() + 1} Training Year` `` —
so the hyphenated two-calendar-year wording is generated by default, and
`SetupPanel`'s step 1 is literally "create planning year" (`SetupPanel.tsx:73`).

## 7. What guided setup currently does

`GuidedYearSetupModal.tsx` opens on a choice of
`method: "new" | "rollover"` (line 45), defaulting to `rollover` when a previous
year exists, and calls `planningApi.rolloverYear(...)` (line 54). Year creation
is step one of the guided experience.

## 8. What the main TMS does

Holds the year selector and the year-management surface: create, rename,
archive, restore, rollover, export. Year-scoped pages read a selected
`planning_year_id`.

## 9. What Planning Workspace does

`PlanningWorkspace.tsx` persists **`aafc_pw_year_id`** — a UUID — in
`localStorage` (lines 3, 75, 80) and initialises from it so year-scoped queries
fire immediately. It resolves a requested year by matching against the fetched
list, `years.find(y => y.year === reqYear)` (line 123), and carries a guard for
a stale stored id (comment at line 287, "DEF-02").

## 10. How year context passes between them

Not by year. TMS links to PW via a configured URL (`S.pwUrl`,
`index.html:5421,5918`) plus a session token fragment. PW then re-derives its own
year from `localStorage` or from its own list fetch. The two share a squadron and
a session, not a year context. See `tms-pw-current-state.md` §4.

## 11. What breaks if no row exists

- Every `/years/{id}/…` endpoint 404s (`_get_year_or_404`).
- Getting Started reports the squadron as not set up (`setup.py:71`).
- Both frontends have no year to select, so year-scoped pages have nothing to
  load.
- Parade night creation cannot link to a year:
  `_find_or_create_parade_date_for_night` (`training.py:504`) returns `None` when
  `_year_for_date` finds nothing, and the night is created invisible to PW —
  the defect recorded as REM-139.

## 12. What depends on `planning_year_id`

The nine FKs in §2, plus `ParadeNight.training_year` — a **bare integer**
(`models/training.py:46`) that duplicates the year concept without referencing
the table, and which the ParadeDate/ParadeNight merge design proposes to drop.

## 13. Duplicate-year failures that have occurred

| id | what happened |
|---|---|
| REM-134 | No uniqueness on `(unit_id, year)`. Duplicates accumulated; a partial unique index was added, scoped to active rows |
| REM-139 | Parade nights linked to the wrong planning year — `_year_for_date` picked the highest-numbered active year, never the date's |
| REM-145 | Forecast reported unconfigured classes as complete |
| REM-156 | Six squadrons held more than one active year in production |

The index added by REM-134 constrains `(unit_id, year)` among active rows. It
prevents two active *2026* rows but permits an active 2026 **and** an active
2027 — which is how the REM-156 state arose.

## 15. What the open Phase A plan would change

PR #36, **open and unmerged**. No code from it exists on main: `PlanningYear` has
no `status` column, there is no `app/services_year.py`, `Wing` has no `timezone`,
and the Alembic head is unchanged at `e2f3a4b5c6d7`.

It proposes: `active_status` → `status` (`draft|active|archived`) dual-written
through one release; rollover creating a **draft**; lazy promotion on 1 January
in squadron-local time via a new `Wing.timezone`; a unique index of one *active*
year per squadron; a frontend notice when the year changes; migrating 296
`active_status` references; and dropping the boolean in a later release.

The superseding instruction challenges the lifecycle premise underneath all of
it. The timezone work and the year-selection tests are the parts most likely to
survive.
