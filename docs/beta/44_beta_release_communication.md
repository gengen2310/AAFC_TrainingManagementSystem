## Beta Release Announcement

**Subject: AAFC TMS Beta Access — Training Management System and Planning Workspace**

---

The AAFC Training Management System (TMS) beta is now available for your squadron.

**Access**

Main TMS: [OFFICIAL LINK TO BE CONFIRMED — do not distribute until production deployment is complete and smoke test passes]

Your unit access code will be provided separately through a secure channel. Do not share your access code with anyone outside your unit.

---

**Logging In**

1. Open the link above in a modern browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Safari recommended)
2. Enter your unit access code when prompted
3. You will land on your unit's Dashboard

**If you use the Planning Workspace** (the planning calendar): it opens automatically within the main TMS — you do not need a separate login.

---

**Supported Browsers**

- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Microsoft Edge
- Safari (Mac/iOS)
- Firefox

Internet Explorer is not supported.

---

**Moving Between TMS and Planning Workspace**

The Planning Workspace is embedded within the main TMS. Access it from the sidebar or menu. You do not need to log in again — your session carries over.

---

**Saving and Autosave**

Most planning actions save automatically. Where a Save button is shown, click it to confirm. If you see a save error, refresh the page and check whether your action was recorded.

Your data is stored on the server — you do not need to download or back up anything locally.

---

**Authoritative Data**

- Curriculum items at the National and Wing level are managed by NATHQ and Wing HQ respectively. Your unit cannot edit them, but can view, review, and schedule them.
- CEA activities are imported from the CAF CEA system. Your unit reviews and classifies them.
- Local activities (parade night–specific entries) are owned by your unit.
- Audit records are permanent and cannot be altered.

---

**Known Limitations in Beta**

The following limitations are acknowledged and documented:

1. **Rooms**: Rooms added via the TMS Resources page are separate from rooms in the Planning Workspace. You may need to add them in both places. This will be unified in a future update.
2. **Facilitators**: Facilitators added via the TMS Facilitators page are separate from those in the Planning Workspace. Add them in both places if using the full planning workflow.
3. **100-user concurrent load**: The system has not yet been tested under full concurrent load. Performance during initial access distribution will be monitored closely.
4. **No mobile optimisation**: The TMS is designed for laptops and desktops. Mobile browsers may work but are not officially supported.

---

**What Must Not Be Entered During Beta**

- **Real cadet names**: Use rank and role (e.g. "Cadet [Surname]") or synthetic names during beta testing
- **Real access codes from other units**: Each unit's code is confidential to that unit
- **Financial information**: The TMS does not support financial records
- **Medical or personal information beyond training-relevant details**: Keep records to the minimum necessary

**Beta Expectations**

This is a supervised beta. You may encounter:
- Minor display issues in some browsers
- Slower responses during peak hours
- Occasional planned maintenance windows (we will notify in advance)

Please continue your normal training planning activities. The system is operational and your data is backed up daily.
